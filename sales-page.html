The Fix

Achievements and salary are not the same problem.

This is the one distinction almost no other resume guide makes — and it's the whole reason this kit exists.

Gets it wrong
"TWD 45,000 starting salary → SGD 1,800"

A direct currency conversion of salary imports a cost-of-living distortion. It doesn't inform the recruiter — it understates you.

Gets it right
"TWD 45,000 — top 20% of entry-level salaries in the Taiwan market"

Market-percentile framing tells the recruiter what the number actually means, instead of asking them to silently (and incorrectly) benchmark it against SGD norms.

Achievement & revenue figures convert directly to SGD — they're market-priced. Salary figures don't — they're priced against local cost of living.
